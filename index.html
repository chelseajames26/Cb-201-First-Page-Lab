The frog is the national animal of Puerto Rico

Frogs are small animals that can live both in water and on land. They belong to a group of animals called amphibians. Most frogs have smooth, wet skin, big eyes, and long back legs that help them jump very far.Frogs have an amazing life cycle.

They start as soft eggs in the water, hatch into swimming tadpoles with tails, and slowly grow legs to become adult frogs. Interestingly, frogs do not drink water through their mouths.

Instead, they soak up water directly through their thin skin.You can find frogs on almost every continent on Earth, except for Antarctica. They eat many kinds of bugs, which helps keep the insect population under control.
Today, many frog types face danger because of pollution and the loss of their natural homes
